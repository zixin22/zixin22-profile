I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia, advised by Professor Zhen Xiang. I joined UGA in Fall 2025.

My research interests center on building secure and trustworthy AI systems, with a focus on LLM agents, AI security, and trustworthy AI. I am especially interested in how agentic systems behave under adversarial or unreliable conditions, and how to make them safer, more robust, and more useful in real-world settings.

Research Interests
======

- LLM Agents
- AI Security
- Trustworthy AI
